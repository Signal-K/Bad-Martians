# Playtest feedback: too much, too soon (2026-11-26)

Source: a tester's messages about martians.playlandnam.space.

> the level of complexity from the get go is too high
> i open this and its too much text before i get to game mechanics
> each level needs to build more gradually and i think complexity can be
> approximated in the amount of text one has to read
> you need a child or someone to do a play through

## Rules this sets

1. **A new player builds and drives before reading anything.** On a first open
   (`FirstOpen`: tutorial not done, no mission won) the game skips the map and
   opens the first mission's rover bay.
2. **Systems appear when earned** (`DisclosureTier`, `game/disclosure.go`):
   - no win: the map shows the title, one chapter and one sentence about it;
   - 1 win: stars and points, the daily mission, the account;
   - 3 wins: the expedition and its one-budget economy.
3. **Mission 1 teaches four parts**, not five: the grip motor joins later.
4. **Text is the complexity proxy.** `TestSceneWordBudgets` caps the words each
   scene draws. Raise a cap only on purpose and say why here.
5. The expedition briefing says its three rules once, with no repeats.

## Still open

- Watch a child or complete newcomer play through, and record where they stall.
- Personalise pacing: raise or lower tiers from how a player actually performs.
- Decide whether the three-win expedition threshold is right.
